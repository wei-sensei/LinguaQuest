🌏 LinguaQuest

Learn languages. Build confidence. Grow through practice.

LinguaQuest is an open-development project exploring how adaptive learning, meaningful language practice and game-based motivation can make language learning more engaging and accessible.

The platform is designed for multiple languages, with Chinese (Mandarin) and Japanese as the initial focus. Its long-term goal is to support additional languages, writing systems, proficiency levels and educational pathways.

🎯 Why LinguaQuest?

Language learning involves much more than memorising vocabulary. Learners need opportunities to recognise, understand, recall and use language in meaningful contexts.

LinguaQuest aims to bring these processes together in one learning environment, helping students build confidence while giving teachers greater insight into their progress.

The project is guided by three principles:

Learning comes first: Game mechanics should reinforce genuine language development.

Progress is personal: Learners develop at different rates and need targeted support.

Teachers remain in control: Technology should support professional judgement, not replace it.

✨ Planned Features

🧠 Adaptive Vocabulary Learning

Spaced repetition that adapts to individual performance.

Vocabulary practice using audio, written language, meanings and context.

Targeted revision of difficult or frequently confused words.

Support for vocabulary, expressions, collocations and grammar.

🗣️ Four-Skill Development

Learning activities will address four interconnected skills:

Listening: Recognising and understanding spoken language.

Reading: Understanding written language and meaning in context.

Writing: Producing vocabulary, sentences and connected texts.

Speaking: Practising spoken communication and building confidence.

The platform will distinguish between completed practice, automatically verified responses and teacher-assessed performance.

👩‍🏫 Teacher-Managed Learning

Teachers will be able to:

Create classes and manage vocabulary collections.

Organise learning by topic, unit and proficiency level.

Set language expectations and differentiate activities.

Monitor student progress through visual dashboards.

Identify areas where learners need additional support.

Independent learners are also part of the longer-term vision.

🐉 Creature-Based Learning Adventures

LinguaQuest will introduce original collectible creatures linked to language-learning challenges.

Learners will be able to encounter, collect and evolve creatures through meaningful practice and demonstrated progress.

The goal is to encourage curiosity, persistence and a sense of achievement—without relying on public academic rankings or punitive streaks.

🌐 Multilingual by Design

Chinese and Japanese will be the first languages developed, but the underlying platform will be designed to accommodate other languages.

Language-specific learning tools, such as Pinyin, furigana, Hanzi and Kanji support, will be developed as modular features rather than assumptions built into the entire system.

🛠️ Technology and Development

The proposed technology stack includes:

Next.js and TypeScript for web application development.

PostgreSQL for structured learning data.

Tailwind CSS for the user interface.

Docker for portable, self-hostable deployment.

Modular language components to support future expansion.

The project follows a Free-First development strategy, aiming to minimise ongoing costs and avoid mandatory paid AI services.

More advanced AI-assisted speaking, writing feedback and interactive conversations may be explored in later development stages, subject to funding, reliability and privacy requirements.

🚧 Project Status

Status: Early development / planning

LinguaQuest is currently being designed and developed. The features described above represent the intended direction of the project and should not be interpreted as completed functionality.

Initial development will focus on establishing a reliable foundation, including learner accounts, vocabulary management, adaptive revision and essential learning activities.

🤝 Who Is This Project For?

LinguaQuest is being developed with the needs of secondary-school language learners and teachers in mind, but its design is intended to be flexible enough for broader educational use.

The project may be of interest to:

Language teachers interested in adaptive and differentiated learning.

Students seeking more engaging ways to practise languages.

Developers interested in educational technology.

Educators and researchers exploring gamification and language acquisition.

🔒 Privacy and Responsible Development

Student privacy, data protection and responsible use of technology are important design considerations.

Development will initially use fictional or synthetic learner data. Any deployment involving real students will require appropriate institutional approval, security safeguards and privacy arrangements.

🌱 Long-Term Vision

The long-term ambition is to develop LinguaQuest into an accessible, multilingual learning environment that combines evidence-informed practice, teacher expertise, learner independence and enjoyable game-based progression.

The aim is not simply to make language learning more entertaining, but to make meaningful practice something learners want to return to.

LinguaQuest is an evolving educational technology project. Features, architecture and development priorities may change as the project progresses.
